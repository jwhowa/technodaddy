About Technodaddy

This is not a corporate LMS and it is not startup theater. It is a grey-beard teacher who has lived the work and knows how to make it clear.

Where it started

Jim’s career began on the floor with a screwdriver, building and installing 486 machines when “IT” still meant ribbon cables and dust. That hands-on start never left. If you cannot explain a system without the buzzwords, you do not own it yet.

Where he is now

As Director of Enablement at Radiology Partners, Jim helps real people adopt complex systems without drowning in jargon. Technodaddy brings that same craft to everyone else: older adults, curious beginners, and teams that are just getting started with AI.
